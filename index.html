About

Alex founded SignalPath in August 2026 with the mission of helping mature the custom integration industry by drawing upon enterprise frameworks, discipline, and operating models—tailoring them for the CEDIA channel and guiding organizations to implement them to create more value for their businesses, partners, and clients.

With 15 years of leadership experience in the CEDIA channel on both the manufacturing and integration sides of the industry, Alex brings a diverse skill set spanning a variety of functions, including sales, marketing, customer experience, operations, procurement, and finance.

Over his career, Alex has played key roles in growing two category-defining startups into CEDIA Hall-of-Fame manufacturers and service providers, worked closely with industry buying groups as both a manufacturer and a dealer, and held a variety of cross-functional leadership positions at a PE-backed roll-up of 20 owner-operated integration firms into a national platform. This experience has given him a unique perspective on the industry, and he brings that insight and context to every SignalPath engagement.

Eighteen years of an industry top-100 ranking · R Markdown · Google Data Analytics Capstone

The custom integration channel's most-cited dataset is a voluntary, self-reported annual ranking of its top 100 firms. Manufacturers use it to size the channel and decide where dealer-program dollars go. This case study compiles all eighteen editions (2009–2026) into a documented, names-stripped dataset and runs three tests — coverage, composition and selection — to ask whether the list can support the decisions people make with it. The short answer: use it to find firms, not to size a market, compute a growth rate, or benchmark a dealer.
